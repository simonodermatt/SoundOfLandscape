I have fixed the vinyl needle animation in `ui.js`.
The user wanted the needle (orange dot) to start at the outer edge (3 o'clock) and slowly move inwards as the vinyl plays, similar to a real turntable.
Previously, the needle was tracking the angle of the spiral, but not properly moving inwards due to incorrect progress interpolation logic, and it also crashed for AI sound presets when `vinylArray` was empty.
I've updated `startVinylDotAnimation` to:
- Use `progress` to linearly interpolate `currentRadius` between `maxRadius` and `minRadius`.
- Force the needle to stay at `fixedAngleRad = 0` (3 o'clock).
- Add robust guards `window.vinylArray && window.vinylArray.length > 0` for calculating min/max Y variations so it works for AI sound presets without crashing.
